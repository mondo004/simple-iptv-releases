Privacy policy

Simple IPTV · last updated 16 September 2026

Simple IPTV is a personal, non-commercial app. There is no Simple IPTV server, account or analytics service. The developer does not receive, store or see any of your data.

What stays on your TV

The playlists and guide addresses you add (including any usernames and passwords for them), your favorites, hidden and reordered channels, watch progress and settings are stored on the device. The app connects directly to the playlist and guide providers you configure, and to GitHub to check for app updates.

Google Drive (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app asks for one limited permission, drive.file: access only to the files and folders this app creates. It keeps them in a Simple IPTV folder in your Drive — backups in Backups, and the sync files that keep your TVs in step in Sync. The app cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.

Sync files and backups are encrypted on the TV with a passphrase you choose before they are uploaded, so they cannot be read without it. The app also reads your Google account's email address and free storage space from Drive, to show which account a TV is using. This data goes only to your own Google Drive; it is not sent anywhere else, shared, sold, or used for advertising.

Simple IPTV's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Removing Google Drive from a TV's storage settings deletes that TV's sign-in token. To remove access for every TV at once, revoke it at myaccount.google.com/connections. Deleting the Simple IPTV folder from your Drive removes everything the app stored there. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the TV.
