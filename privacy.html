Privacy policy

Simple IPTV · last updated 16 September 2026

Simple IPTV is a personal, non-commercial app. There is no Simple IPTV server, account or analytics service. The developer does not receive, store or see any of your data.

What stays on your TV

The playlists and guide addresses you add (including any usernames and passwords for them), your favorites, hidden and reordered channels, watch progress and settings are stored on the device. The app connects directly to the playlist and guide providers you configure, and to GitHub to check for app updates.

Google Drive (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app asks for two limited permissions:

  • drive.appdata — a hidden folder in your Drive that only this app can use. It holds the sync files that keep your TVs in step.
  • drive.file — only files and folders this app creates, such as a visible Simple IPTV backup folder. The app cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.

Sync files are encrypted on the TV with a passphrase you choose before they are uploaded, so they cannot be read without it. Backups are encrypted too when you set a passphrase. This data goes only to your own Google Drive; it is not sent anywhere else, shared, sold, or used for advertising.

Simple IPTV's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Disconnecting Google Drive in the app's settings deletes the sign-in token from the TV. You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections, and delete the hidden sync data from Google Drive's settings under Manage apps. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the TV.
