Simple IPTV

A live TV guide and player for Android TV boxes.

Simple IPTV plays the IPTV playlists you add to it, shows a programme guide, and keeps your favorites, hidden channels and watch progress. It is a personal project shared with friends and family; it is not sold and has no ads.

Optionally, you can connect your own Google Drive so several TVs in your home share the same favorites and settings, and so you can keep backups. The app only ever sees the files it created there itself.
